Bell & Corner

A fight bet that settles itself. No bookmaker, no escrow, no custody.

Betting on a fight normally means trusting whoever holds the money. A Discreet Log Contract removes that party. Two people lock funds, an oracle publishes one signature saying who won, and that signature is the only thing needed to release the money to the right person.

The oracle never touches the funds, never learns the contract exists, and cannot pay the wrong person even if it wanted to. This page implements that mechanism and lets you run it: BIP-340 oracle attestation, anticipation points and Schnorr adaptor signatures, all computed in this tab. No server, no node, no wallet.

1 · Announce the fight

Before the fight, the oracle publishes a public key and a one-time nonce, and names the possible outcomes. That is the whole announcement. It says nothing about who will win and nothing about any bet.

2 · What the bettors can compute in advance

From that announcement alone, anyone can work out the exact point the oracle's future signature will land on, for each outcome. These are the anticipation points. They can be computed now, but nobody can produce the matching signature until the oracle chooses.

Publish an announcement first.

3 · Lock the bet

Each side signs a payout, but encrypted to its outcome's anticipation point. The result is an adaptor signature: provably correct, and useless until the matching oracle signature exists.

4 · The fight ends

The oracle signs one outcome and publishes a single number. It does not know who bet, how much, or that this contract exists.

Why the oracle cannot cheat

It signs an outcome, not a payout. The same signature settles every contract on that fight anywhere in the world, and settles none of them differently. It cannot pay the loser, because the loser's adaptor signature only completes with a number the oracle did not publish. It cannot steal, because it never held anything.

Implemented: BIP-340 oracle attestation, anticipation point derivation, Schnorr adaptor signature creation and completion, and verification that the wrong outcome fails to complete. Not implemented: funding transactions, CET construction and broadcast. This page proves the settlement mechanism; it does not move coins.
